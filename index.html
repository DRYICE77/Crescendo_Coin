HOW CRESCENDO WORKS

Stream. Grow. Earn.

We turn measurable streaming growth into a Stream Score. Artists climb the leaderboard based on their performance, and a future rewards engine could allocate CRES tokens from a defined pool. Smaller artists can compete on growth—not just raw audience size.

*CRES amounts are hypothetical reward estimates for demonstration. They are not claimable, issued, or guaranteed, and token economics have not been finalized.
PROOF OF CONCEPT

Powered by Soundcharts

Spotify cumulative stream observations are used to calculate 7-day, 14-day, and 30-day differences. Artist and song details resolve directly from Spotify track IDs in live mode.
